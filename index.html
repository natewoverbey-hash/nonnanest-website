Questions

Things parents actually ask us

No jargon, no runaround. Just clear answers.

Does my baby need to wear anything?

No. Nonnanest uses passive infrared sensors to read your baby's skin temperature from up to 4 feet away. Nothing touches your baby — no socks, no ankle bands, no clips, no straps. Mount the camera directly above the crib and it does the rest. Every other device that tracks wellness insights requires a wearable — Nonnanest is the only one that doesn't.

Does Nonnanest need WiFi?

No. Nonnanest communicates directly between the nursery camera and parent handheld using FHSS (Frequency Hopping Spread Spectrum) encryption. No WiFi, no cloud, no internet dependency. Private by design, not by promise.

How is Nonnanest different from wearable monitors?

Every other device that tracks wellness insights requires your baby to wear a sock or ankle band. Nonnanest is the only monitor that senses skin temperature contactlessly — nothing touches your baby. We also don't require WiFi, don't need an app or phone, and have no subscriptions. We're built for parents who want skin temperature awareness without putting anything on their baby.

What does Nonnanest actually measure?

Surface skin temperature, from up to four feet away, with no wearable and nothing touching your baby. It also reads the room temperature, because the room affects the skin. Both show on the display.

How accurate is the skin temperature reading?

We test every reading against a NIST-traceable blackbody calibration source, the same kind of reference used to check professional and medical infrared instruments. Nonnanest is calibrated to work within recommended ambient temperatures of 69–75°F and between 3 and 4 feet from baby, at a safe distance. While it works closer than 3 feet, pediatric guidance and our own guidance is to always maintain a 3-foot distance from baby.

Why is skin temperature different from what a thermometer reads?

Skin runs cooler than the inside of the body, and how much cooler depends on the room. That's normal, and it's true of every contactless thermometer, including the one at your pediatrician's office. What matters for you isn't the exact gap. It's that skin temp goes up when your baby warms up. That's the signal Nonnanest is built to catch.

Why doesn't the number match my thermometer?

Because they're measuring different things. Your thermometer reads inside. Nonnanest reads the surface. Neither one is wrong. If Nonnanest shows something elevated, that's your cue to reach for the thermometer, not a replacement for it.

What do I do if the reading looks high?

Check your baby with a contact thermometer. SightAware is built to get your attention, not to answer the question. Because every baby's baseline is different, you set your own alert threshold on the handheld, typically 2 to 3°F above your baby's usual reading. If anything concerns you, call your pediatrician.
