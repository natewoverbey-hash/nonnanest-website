Story angles

For journalists and editors. Nathan Overbey is available for interviews, background conversations, and product demos.

SightAware by Nonnanest sits at the intersection of three strong editorial angles: privacy-first technology, founder origin story, and a product gap the baby monitor category has ignored for decades. Each angle below is available for editorial use.

The privacy angle

A baby monitor built to stay offline — on purpose

FHSS connectivity, the closed-loop wireless protocol that keeps all data local, is not new to baby monitors. What is new is combining it with wellness insights. Every product on the market that tracks meaningful health-adjacent data requires an internet connection to function. SightAware is the only monitor that delivers contactless skin temperature awareness entirely offline. No cloud. No app. No account. It's a deliberate choice at a time when every other wellness tech product is moving toward connectivity, not away from it.

The founder angle

A Charleston dad built what the market didn't have

Nathan Overbey built SightAware after a 3am moment with his six-month-old son. His baby woke crying, clearly unwell, and the monitor could only tell him the room temperature. He spent two years researching the gap, sourced and engineered the product while working a full-time job, and used it on his own kids before shipping a single unit. He is a father of two, a 15-year enterprise tech veteran, and a first-time hardware founder based in Charleston, SC.

The product gap angle

Every baby monitor measures the room. Only one measures the baby.

Every major baby monitor on the market measures room temperature. SightAware measures the baby's skin temperature, contactlessly, from up to four feet away, using the same infrared sensing principle as no-touch thermometers. No wearable. No app. No subscription. $329, one time. It is a feature the category has never had in a standalone dedicated monitor.

The gift guide angle

One-time price. No subscription. Ships from the US.

SightAware fits roundups for best baby monitors, tech gifts for new parents, non-WiFi baby monitors, and privacy-first baby gear. It also fits the broader wellness and smart product landscape: gift guides covering smart home wellness, modern parenting tech, and connected health devices that respect privacy. About & founder

About Nonnanest

Nonnanest, Inc. is a baby wellness technology company based in Charleston, SC, founded in 2024 by Nathan Overbey. The company's flagship product, SightAware™ by Nonnanest, is a contactless infrared baby wellness monitor that reads skin temperature from up to four feet away without requiring a wearable, WiFi, or a smartphone app. SightAware communicates via FHSS (Frequency Hopping Spread Spectrum), a secure wireless protocol that keeps all data local between the nursery camera and a dedicated 5-inch HD parent display. Patent pending. Priced at $329 with no subscription fees.

Founder

Nathan Overbey, Founder & CEO

Nathan designed SightAware after realizing no baby monitor on the market could track skin temperature contactlessly. He brings over 15 years of enterprise technology experience from Salesforce, ServiceNow, and Blackbaud, and holds an MBA from the University of South Carolina. Nathan is a father of two and the product's first real-world user.

Founder quotes

Journalists are welcome to use any of the following quotes attributed to Nathan Overbey, Founder & CEO, Nonnanest.

On the origin

When our son was six months old, he woke us up crying in the middle of the night. By the time we got to his room, it was obvious something was wrong. He was clearly unwell. I looked back at the monitor and all it could tell me was the room temperature. I kept thinking: how long had his skin temperature been rising while we slept? That was the moment I realized the market needed more awareness, not another camera.

Nathan Overbey, Founder & CEO, Nonnanest
On privacy

We made a deliberate decision to keep SightAware offline. Parents are concerned about connected devices and security risks. We built it on FHSS, a fully encrypted signal that never touches the internet. Private by design, not by promise.

Nathan Overbey, Founder & CEO, Nonnanest
On the approach

We're not trying to replace a parent's instinct. We're giving that instinct better information. SightAware is a gentle layer of awareness that works quietly in the background while your baby sleeps.

Nathan Overbey, Founder & CEO, Nonnanest
On design philosophy

We're not anti-technology. We're very pro-innovation. But here's what struck me: every wellness insight product on the market today requires an internet connection to function. We made a deliberate choice to go the other way. Every design decision started with one question: does this serve the parent, or does it serve the product?

Nathan Overbey, Founder & CEO, Nonnanest
On the NAPPA recognition

NAPPA reviewers are parents first, testing in their own homes with their own children. That is the audience we built SightAware for. The feedback that stood out most was not about the technology. It was about a monitor that fades into the background of a nursery and gives parents awareness without demanding their attention. That was the design brief from day one.

Nathan Overbey, Founder & CEO, Nonnanest
